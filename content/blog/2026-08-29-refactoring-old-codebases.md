It's been almost 10 years since I had unfettered access to the internet and a computer. My
education and career started in a different field, and slowly I gravitated towards technology. It
was only recently that I got a formal education in building software. I always gravitated to the
next fancy idea without fully finishing anything meaningful.

Recently I came to a realization that the way I was operating wasn't the best. I was mainly a
consumer of the internet, but never a creator.

Right now I have the time and the means to do this properly, and the last couple of years of AI
advancements gave me the confidence to restart everything from scratch. I used to build things very
sporadically, but everything was very unorganized and chaotic. None of the things I built or wrote,
or even my communication for that matter, ever really landed with anyone. But now, with the
available AI tools, I can actually finish some of what I start. They help most with the parts where
I used to stall.

The first task I picked up is to clean up all the old repositories I have built. Some of them
aren't even version tracked lol. I have around 10 different projects which I worked on over the
last few years. The goal is to clean all of those up, find the potentially good ones, assess and
analyze which ones to keep and which ones to discard, and host them on this website.

Are these useful in any manner? Maybe not, but this is definitely a learning opportunity to
understand technology, systems etc at a deeper level. Of course this process is AI-driven, and the
goal is to understand both tech and AI systems with a practical use case (practical only for
myself, not a real world use case).
